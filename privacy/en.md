# Privacy Policy of Tone Studio

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Application keeps the User’s recordings and tones on the User’s own iPhone or iPad. It has no accounts, shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking services, and sends no Data to servers run by the Owner.

* This Application shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking services. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), cookies or similar technologies, and does not track Users across other companies’ apps or websites.

## Types of Data processed

Among the types of Personal Data that this Application processes, there are:

* **Recordings:** the audio captured by the device’s microphone while the User is recording in this Application, which may include the User’s voice and the voices of other people nearby.
* **Imported media:** the audio files and videos that the User picks in Files or Photos, or shares to this Application from another app. This Application receives only the items the User picks, not the rest of the User’s photo library or files. From a video, it keeps only the sound.
* **Tones:** the tones the User saves, with their names, their type (ringtone or text tone), their length, the date they were made, and the settings used to make them in the Composer or the editor.
* **Typed text:** the words the User types for a Talking Tone, to be spoken, and in the Composer’s Describe it, to suggest a tune. They are turned into sound or settings on the User’s device by features built into iOS and are not sent to the Owner.
* **Purchase information:** which Products the User has bought and whether the User’s Tone Studio Premium subscription is active, as provided by Apple.
* **Support information:** the content of any email the User sends to the Owner, and the User’s email address.

Personal Data is provided by the User, for example when recording, importing or writing to the Owner, or is created when this Application is used, for example when the User saves a tone.

Without access to the microphone, which iOS asks the User to allow, this Application cannot record. Everything else in this Application works without it.

Users who are uncertain about which Personal Data is required are welcome to contact the Owner.

Users are responsible for any third-party Personal Data obtained, published or shared through this Application, such as another person’s voice in a recording, and confirm that they have the third party’s consent to record it and to provide it.

## How this Application handles the Data

### Recording and importing

The microphone is used only while the User is recording in this Application. Recordings and imported media are processed on the User’s device, and are stored, together with the tones made from them, in this Application’s own storage on that device. They are not sent to the Owner or to anyone else. Once a tone has been saved, this Application keeps the tone, not the original recording or imported file, as described in “Retention time”.

### Sharing and setting tones

When the User taps Set as Ringtone, or exports a tone in another way, this Application creates an audio file of that tone and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where the User chooses to send it, for example to iOS’s Use as Ringtone action, to Files, by AirDrop or to another app. From then on, the privacy terms of that destination apply.

### Backups

Like the data of other apps, the User’s tones may be included in backups of the device that the User makes with iCloud or with a computer. Such backups are made and kept under the User’s own settings and, for iCloud, under Apple’s privacy policy. The Owner has no access to them.

### Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s own privacy policy. The Owner never receives the User’s name, email address, payment details or Apple Account.

To know what the User has bought, this Application asks Apple’s StoreKit on the User’s device, which returns which tones the User owns, including tones shared through Family Sharing, and whether Tone Studio Premium is active. This Application uses that information only to unlock those tones and Premium. It stays on the User’s device and is not sent to the Owner or anyone else.

### Support emails

Users can write to the Owner at the email address in this document. Settings › Contact support in this Application opens the Owner’s support page, which shows that address. The Owner uses support emails only to answer them.

### Data from Apple

If the User has chosen to share analytics with app developers in the settings of their iPhone or iPad, Apple may provide the Owner with crash reports and anonymous usage statistics about this Application. This is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, and Users can turn it off at any time in those settings.

### Retention time

Personal Data shall be processed and stored for as long as required by the purpose they have been collected for.

Therefore:

* Recordings, imported media and tones stay on the User’s device until the User removes them: by deleting a tone in My Tones, or by deleting this Application.
* The original recordings and imported files that tones are made from are temporary working copies. When a video is imported, the copy of the video is deleted as soon as its sound has been extracted. The remaining working copies are deleted the next time this Application starts, and iOS may remove them sooner. Preview and export files are temporary too, and iOS may remove them at any time.
* Support emails are kept for as long as needed to answer them and to handle any follow-up, unless the law requires the Owner to keep them longer.

The Owner may be obliged to retain Personal Data for a longer period whenever required to do so for the performance of a legal obligation or upon order of an authority.

Once the retention period expires, Personal Data shall be deleted. Therefore, the right to access, the right to erasure, the right to rectification and the right to data portability cannot be enforced after expiration of the retention period.

* Receive their Data and have it transferred to another controller. Users have the right to receive their Data in a structured, commonly used and machine readable format and, if technically feasible, to have it transmitted to another controller without any hindrance. This provision is applicable provided that the Data is processed by automated means and that the processing is based on the User’s consent, on a contract which the User is part of or on pre-contractual obligations thereof.
