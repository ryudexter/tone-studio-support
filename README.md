# Tone Studio

Make your own ringtones and text tones on iPhone: record a sound, take one from a video or a file, or compose an original tune with the Composer. It needs iOS 18 or later, and also runs on iPad.

This page is Tone Studio's support page. The app's code isn't here.

Tone Studio has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your recordings and tones stay on your iPhone.

## Common questions

**How do I set a tone as my ringtone?** On iOS 26 or later, open the tone in My Tones, tap **Set as Ringtone**, then choose **Use as Ringtone** in the share sheet. iOS adds it to your ringtones in Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone. For a text tone, go to Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Text Tone and pick it from the Ringtones list. Apps can't change your ringtone themselves, so this last step is always iOS's own.

**I don't see Use as Ringtone.** It's in iOS 26 and later, in the share sheet's list of actions below the apps; scroll down if you don't see it. On older iOS, tap **Set as Ringtone**, then **Save to Files**, and finish in GarageBand, Apple's free app: Tone Studio's Settings › How to set a ringtone walks you through it.

**Can I add a tone with my Mac instead?** Yes. In My Tones, open the tone and tap **.m4r for Mac**, and AirDrop the file to your Mac. Connect your iPhone, select it in the Finder sidebar, and drag the .m4r file onto the iPhone's window.

**How do I give someone their own ringtone?** Add the tone to iOS first, as above. Then open Contacts, choose the person, tap Edit, and tap Ringtone or Text Tone.

**Why can't my tone be longer than 30 seconds?** iOS only accepts custom tones up to 30 seconds long, so Tone Studio keeps every tone under that. You can record for up to 90 seconds and trim it to the part you want. Text tones start at 4 seconds.

**Recording doesn't work.** Tone Studio needs the microphone. On the record screen, tap **Open Settings**, or go to the iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn on Tone Studio.

**What can I make a tone from?** Your own recordings, audio files from Files and iCloud Drive, audio files that other apps open in Tone Studio, and the sound from videos in Photos. Songs from Apple Music are copy-protected, so they can't be imported.

**What's free?** Every Starter Tone, the first ringtone and the first text tone of every pack in Discover, recording and importing, the Editor's basic effects, three Composer instruments (Marimba, Pluck and Bell), three voice effects, Charging Sounds, and up to 3 of your own tones (recordings, imports and Composer creations). Tones from Discover don't count toward the 3.

**Can I buy just one tone?** Yes. Tap the price next to any tone in Discover. A ringtone or text tone you buy is a one-time purchase: it's yours on all your devices, and Family Sharing works with it.

**What does Premium add?** Tone Studio Premium is a yearly subscription. It unlocks every tone in Discover, including ones added later, unlimited tones of your own, every Composer instrument and voice effect, Describe it, Talking Tones and Merge & Mix. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe. It renews each year until you cancel. When Premium ends, tones you bought stay yours, and tones already in My Tones stay there.

**How do I cancel Premium?** In Tone Studio, go to Settings › Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Tone Studio Premium › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Premium stays on until the end of the year you paid for.

**I bought something but it isn't showing.** In Tone Studio's Settings, tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I get a refund?** Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**If I delete the app, are my tones gone?** Your tones are stored only in Tone Studio on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them. Your purchases come back with Restore Purchases, and you can add tones from Discover again. Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup includes Tone Studio's tones, as it does other apps' data.

**Can I share or sell the tones I make?**
- **Your own recordings:** yours.
- **Composer tunes:** you can use them for anything. They aren't exclusive: someone else could make the same tune.
- **Tones from Discover:** for your own ringtones and alerts only, not for selling or sharing.
- **Tones made from other people's music:** don't share or sell them without the rights holder's permission.
