# Stiffler

Make your own ringtones and text tones on iPhone: record a sound, take one from a video or a file, or create an original tune in Tone Studio. It needs iOS 18 or later, and also runs on iPad.

This page is Stiffler's support page. The app's code isn't here.

Stiffler has no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions and no tracking. Your recordings and tones stay on your iPhone.

## Common questions

**How do I set a tone as my ringtone?** On iOS 26 or later, open the tone in My Tones, tap **Set as Ringtone**, then choose **Use as Ringtone** in the share sheet. iOS adds it to your ringtones in Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone. For a text tone, go to Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Text Tone and pick it from the Ringtones list. Apps can't change your ringtone themselves, so this last step is always iOS's own.

**I don't see Use as Ringtone.** It's in iOS 26 and later, in the share sheet's list of actions below the apps; scroll down if you don't see it. On older iOS, tap **Set as Ringtone**, then **Save to Files**, and finish in GarageBand, Apple's free app: Stiffler's Settings › How to set a ringtone walks you through it.

**Can I add a tone with my Mac instead?** Yes. In My Tones, open the tone and tap **.m4r for Mac**, and AirDrop the file to your Mac. Connect your iPhone, select it in the Finder sidebar, and drag the .m4r file onto the iPhone's window.

**How do I give someone their own ringtone?** Add the tone to iOS first, as above. Then open Contacts, choose the person, tap Edit, and tap Ringtone or Text Tone.

**Why can't my tone be longer than 30 seconds?** iOS only accepts custom tones up to 30 seconds long, so Stiffler keeps every tone under that. You can record for up to 90 seconds and trim it to the part you want. Text tones start at 4 seconds.

**Recording doesn't work.** Stiffler needs the microphone. On the record screen, tap **Open Settings**, or go to the iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn on Stiffler.

**What can I make a tone from?** Your own recordings, audio files from Files and iCloud Drive, sounds shared to Stiffler from other apps such as Voice Memos, and the sound from videos in Photos. Songs from Apple Music are copy-protected, so they can't be imported.

**What's free, and what does Pro add?** Free: the Starter pack, three Tone Studio instruments (Marimba, Pluck and Bell), and up to 3 of your own tones (recordings, imports and Tone Studio creations). Tones from Discover don't count toward the 3. Pro is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited tones of your own, all 8 instruments and every tone pack, including packs added later. You can also buy packs one at a time. There are no subscriptions, and Family Sharing works with every purchase.

**I bought something but it isn't showing.** In Stiffler's Settings, tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

**How do I get a refund?** Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**If I delete the app, are my tones gone?** Your tones are stored only in Stiffler on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them. Your purchases come back with Restore Purchases, and you can add tones from Discover again. Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup includes Stiffler's tones, as it does other apps' data.

**Can I share or sell the tones I make?**
- **Your own recordings:** yours.
- **Tone Studio tunes:** you can use them for anything. They aren't exclusive: someone else could make the same tune.
- **Tones from Discover:** for your own ringtones and alerts only, not for selling or sharing.
- **Tones made from other people's music:** don't share or sell them without the rights holder's permission.
